ROCK
FINGERS

Play real songs with your hands. The app knows the chords, so you just keep time. Your webcam stays on your computer.

  1. Pick a song at the top, or add your own by typing its chords. New to it? Hit 🎓 TRAIN for step-by-step lessons.
  2. Pinch (thumb + index) with your play hand to play the next chord. Hold the pinch to keep it ringing.
  3. Other hand: raise it for a brighter tone, pinch to go back a chord, make a fist to stop.
  4. Know the song? Switch to PADS and pick the chords yourself, with the next one glowing.
